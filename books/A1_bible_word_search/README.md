# A1 - Bible Word Search Large Print

A1 has moved to the **v3 60-puzzle retail interior** and is the current design-locked candidate.

## Current build

- Trim: 8.5 x 11 in
- Interior: black-and-white, no bleed
- Page count: 104
- Puzzle count: 60
- Grid: 16 x 16
- Main words per puzzle: 15
- Difficulty mix: 42 Relaxed / 12 Medium / 6 Challenge
- Bonus challenges: 6
- Solutions: 30 pages, two enlarged solutions per page
- Bible-focus reference + original context on every puzzle
- Completion tracking on every puzzle
- Three-page large-type puzzle index
- Five section-opening pages
- Two closing/reflection pages
- Embedded fonts only

## Five equal sections

Each section contains exactly 12 puzzles:

1. Beginnings & Foundations - puzzles 1-12
2. Heroes, Kings & Prophets - puzzles 13-24
3. The Life & Teachings of Jesus - puzzles 25-36
4. The Early Church & Mission - puzzles 37-48
5. Letters, Revelation & Faith - puzzles 49-60

## Why v3 reduced the puzzle count

The product now favors curation and readability over raw quantity. Twenty weaker, redundant, or list-heavy themes were removed. The freed page budget was used for larger index/support typography, readable two-up solutions, section previews, and closing/reflection pages.

## Additional v3 improvements

- Challenge puzzles now contain materially more backwards words, diagonals, and overlap than Medium puzzles.
- Long biblical names remain genuinely large through an embedded condensed-bold word-list font.
- James (Zebedee) and James (Alphaeus) are distinguished.
- Daniel includes Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego instead of the generic Three Friends.
- Camel Hair was corrected to Camel's Hair.
- New Testament Overview was replaced by Armor of God (Ephesians 6:10-20).
- Fruit of the Spirit & Christian Virtues explicitly distinguishes the nine named qualities from related virtues.
- The non-embedded Helvetica resource from v2 has been eliminated.

## Production method

1. Curated theme, reference, context, and vocabulary data.
2. Deterministic algorithmic 16 x 16 grid generation.
3. Exact coordinates stored for every answer.
4. Six Challenge puzzles place one additional named bonus word.
5. Every intended answer path is independently validated.
6. Accidental duplicate intended-word occurrences are rejected.
7. Duplicate grids are rejected.
8. Solutions are generated from stored coordinates.
9. Layout-risk checks verify all context blocks and word-list entries fit target type sizes.
10. PDF is rendered and visually sampled before proofing.
