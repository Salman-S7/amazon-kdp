# A1 v2 Editorial Architecture

## Product idea

The v2 interior is designed as **a relaxing large-print journey through the Bible**, not merely a collection of Bible-themed word lists.

Each puzzle now contains:
- one clear theme
- 15 curated words
- a Bible-focus reference
- one original context sentence
- a visible difficulty level
- completion tracking
- an enlarged answer key

Eight Challenge puzzles also include one extra named bonus word.

## Section architecture

### Part 1 - Beginnings & Foundations
Puzzles 1-14. Creation, patriarchs, Exodus, covenant, tabernacle, Joshua, and Jericho.

### Part 2 - Heroes, Kings & Prophets
Puzzles 15-41. Judges, Ruth, Samuel, Saul, David, Solomon, prophets, exile-era material, wisdom books, Jerusalem, and an Old Testament overview.

### Part 3 - The Life & Teachings of Jesus
Puzzles 42-56. Bethlehem, Galilee, Gospels, birth, John the Baptist, disciples, ministry, miracles, parables, Sermon on the Mount, prayer, Last Supper, crucifixion, resurrection, and Great Commission.

### Part 4 - The Early Church & Mission
Puzzles 57-62. Acts, Pentecost, early church, Peter, Paul, and Paul's journeys.

### Part 5 - Letters, Revelation & Faith
Puzzles 63-80. New Testament letters, Revelation, New Testament overview, and reflective themes such as faith, hope, love, gratitude, peace, courage, and Christian virtues.

## Bonus challenge positions

Challenge puzzles 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, and 80 include a sixteenth hidden answer displayed separately from the main word list.

## Editorial standard

Prefer specific people, places, actions, and images over generic filler. Do not imply that related Christian concepts are part of a canonical biblical list when they are not. Use references and original summaries rather than copying modern Bible-translation text.
