# A1 v3 - 60-Puzzle Editorial Architecture

## Product idea

**A relaxing, genuinely readable large-print journey through the Bible.**

The 60-puzzle edition deliberately prioritizes stronger themes, larger functional typography, and clearer progression over maximum puzzle count.

## Section architecture

### Part 1 - Beginnings & Foundations
Puzzles 1-12: Genesis Beginnings, Garden of Eden, Noah, Abraham, Jacob, Joseph, Moses, Exodus, Ten Commandments, Tabernacle, Joshua, Jericho.

### Part 2 - Heroes, Kings & Prophets
Puzzles 13-24: Ruth, Samuel, David, David and Goliath, Solomon, Elijah, Esther, Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Daniel, Jonah.

### Part 3 - The Life & Teachings of Jesus
Puzzles 25-36: Birth of Jesus, John the Baptist, Twelve Disciples, Jesus' Ministry, Miracles, Parables, Sermon on the Mount, Prayer, Last Supper, Crucifixion, Resurrection, Great Commission.

### Part 4 - The Early Church & Mission
Puzzles 37-48: Acts, Pentecost, Early Church, Peter, Paul, Paul's Journeys, Romans, Corinthians, Galatians and Ephesians, Prison Letters, Pastoral Letters, James.

### Part 5 - Letters, Revelation & Faith
Puzzles 49-60: Hebrews, Peter's Letters, John's Letters, Revelation, Armor of God, Faith, Hope, Love, Gratitude, Peace, Courage, Fruit of the Spirit & Christian Virtues.

## Difficulty architecture

- 42 Relaxed (70%)
- 12 Medium (20%)
- 6 Challenge (10%)

Challenge positions: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60. Each has one additional bonus word.

## Page architecture

- 7 front/index pages
- 5 section openers
- 60 puzzle pages
- 30 two-up solution pages
- 2 closing/reflection pages
- 104 pages total

## Editorial standard

Prefer specific people, places, actions, and images over generic filler. Avoid weak duplication between neighboring puzzles. Use references and original summaries instead of reproducing modern Bible-translation text. Keep the main puzzle experience calm and uncluttered.
