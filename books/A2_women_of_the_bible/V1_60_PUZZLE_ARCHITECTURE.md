# A2 v1 - 60-Puzzle Editorial Architecture

## Product idea

**A relaxing, genuinely readable large-print journey through the women of the Bible.**

A2 keeps the proven A1 retail structure while shifting the editorial experience toward individual women, relationships, acts of courage, wisdom, hospitality, prayer, witness, and service.

## Difficulty architecture

- 42 Relaxed (70%)
- 12 Medium (20%)
- 6 Challenge (10%)

Challenge positions: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60.

Each Challenge puzzle gets:
- materially more backwards words, diagonals, and overlap
- one additional named bonus word
- the same large-print usability standard as the rest of the book

## Page architecture

Use the A1 retail structure as the baseline:

- 7 front/index pages
- 5 section openers
- 60 puzzle pages
- 30 two-up solution pages
- 2 closing/reflection pages
- 104 pages total

## Uniqueness rule vs A1

Target at least 80% unique vocabulary/theme combinations compared with A1.

Direct theme overlap should be minimal. Ruth and Esther may reappear because they are essential to this title, but their A2 vocabulary must focus on the woman's own narrative rather than reusing A1 word sets.

Other women who appeared incidentally inside A1 topics should receive newly curated vocabulary centered on their own passages.

## Editorial standard

Prefer:
- named people
- family relationships
- places
- concrete actions
- objects
- occupations
- decisions
- narrative images

Avoid:
- generic inspirational filler
- repetitive virtue lists
- unsupported biographical claims
- flattening difficult stories into simplistic moral lessons
- reproducing modern copyrighted Bible wording

Every puzzle should include:
1. a clear title
2. a Bible-focus reference
3. an original 1-3 sentence context note
4. 15 validated main words
5. exact generated coordinates
6. difficulty label
7. completion tracker

## Human-review flags

Give special editorial review to:
- Tamar of Genesis
- Michal
- Bathsheba
- Vashti
- Noadiah
- the Canaanite Woman
- Euodia & Syntyche

The goal is factual, respectful, text-grounded treatment without importing unnecessary doctrinal or cultural claims.
