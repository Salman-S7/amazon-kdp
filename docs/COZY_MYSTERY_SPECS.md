# Cozy Mystery Series Specifications

## Objective

Build one bingeable cozy-mystery series with recurring characters, one recognizable setting, and a new fully resolved mystery in each book.

Initial authorization:
- B1 — Series Entry
- B2 — Read-Through Book
- B3 — Validation Book

Scale only after reader data:
- B4–B6 in the same series

## Recommended manuscript length

- B1: ~65,000–75,000 words
- B2: ~65,000–75,000 words
- B3: ~65,000–80,000 words

## Before drafting B1

Complete:
1. series bible
2. town map
3. recurring-character database
4. 6–8 book opportunity map
5. B1–B3 mystery concepts
6. recurring relationship arcs
7. tone and reader promise
8. cover/packaging reference board

Do not publish B1 and only then decide what B2 is.

## Series bible

Track for every recurring character:
- name
- age
- appearance
- occupation
- speech/voice notes
- family
- personality
- secrets
- relationships
- first appearance

Track every recurring location:
- name
- owner
- purpose
- physical description
- relative geography
- recurring details

Track:
- timeline
- who knows what
- relationship changes
- injuries/events
- town history
- unresolved personal arcs

## Mystery architecture

Every book must define:
- victim
- killer
- motive
- method
- opportunity
- 4–6 plausible suspects
- real clues
- red herrings
- reveal logic

The solution must feel surprising but fair.

Do not introduce the true killer at the end with no prior setup.

## Book B1

Purpose:
- introduce protagonist
- establish town
- establish central business/hobby
- establish recurring cast
- establish series tone
- solve one complete murder mystery
- seed longer personal arcs

No cliffhanger that withholds the murder solution.

## Book B2

Purpose:
- test whether readers want more of the world
- deliver a new complete mystery
- deepen recurring relationships
- reveal more of the town
- progress one continuing personal arc

## Book B3

Purpose:
- provide the strongest mystery of the pilot
- pay off recurring-character setup
- deepen long-term reader attachment
- create a clear reason to want B4 without leaving the central mystery unresolved

## AI-assisted fiction workflow

1. Market brief
2. Series bible
3. Book-level outline
4. Chapter outline
5. Scene cards
6. Scene-by-scene drafting
7. Continuity QA
8. Mystery-logic QA
9. Repetition/style QA
10. Human rewrite/edit
11. Proofread
12. Format

Each scene card should include:
- POV
- location
- scene goal
- conflict
- clue/information requirement
- emotional change
- opening state
- closing hook

Never ask AI to generate the whole novel in one prompt and publish the raw result.

## Cover rules

The cover is commercial packaging.

A buyer should immediately recognize:
**cozy mystery**

Maintain consistent series branding:
- typography family
- layout logic
- illustration/art approach
- author-name placement
- series badge/treatment

Study current successful covers before designing.

## Formats

Initial:
- Kindle eBook
- paperback

Add hardcover/audio/translation only after demand is proven.

## KDP Select / KU

Initial experiment: enroll the Kindle edition in KDP Select for the first 90-day term, assuming current terms still fit the business at launch.

Re-evaluate after the first term.

## Release cadence

Do not launch B1 without backlog.

Starting cadence:
- B1 → B2: ~3–5 weeks
- B2 → B3: ~3–5 weeks

Quality overrides cadence.

## Read-through success gate

Internal management thresholds:

- 60%+ B1→B2: very strong
- 45–60%: promising
- 30–45%: diagnose before aggressive scaling
- under 30%: pause B4–B6 and investigate positioning/story satisfaction

Investigate:
- cover mismatch
- blurb mismatch
- weak ending
- unsatisfying mystery
- character problems
- poor B2 packaging
- repetitive or AI-sounding prose
