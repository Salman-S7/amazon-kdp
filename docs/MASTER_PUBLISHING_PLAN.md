# AI Publishing Business — Master Execution Plan

## Goal

Build an AI-assisted publishing company, not a PDF-upload operation.

AI may accelerate research, drafting, puzzle generation, illustration, editing, formatting, metadata, QA, and analytics. Every published title must still be original, legally safe, commercially useful, accurately formatted, and human-reviewed.

## Initial portfolio

We will run two publishing engines:

1. **Fast catalog:** Christian/Bible large-print puzzle books.
2. **Compounding IP:** one cozy-mystery fiction series.

The first production authorization is **6 books total**:
- Puzzle A1–A3
- Cozy B1–B3

The next 6 books are earned by performance:
- Puzzle A4–A6
- Cozy B4–B6

Maximum Year-1 core catalog: **12 books**.

## Why this structure

### Puzzle catalog
- fast and inexpensive to test
- highly automatable
- evergreen
- strong print fit
- low factual risk
- series/catalog expansion potential

### Cozy mystery
- repeat readers
- series read-through
- Kindle Unlimited fit
- eBook + paperback economics
- recurring world and characters
- AI is useful for continuity, clues, timelines, and series bibles

## Execution sequence

### Stage 0 — Foundation
1. Configure one KDP account.
2. Complete identity, bank, and tax setup.
3. Establish puzzle imprint/brand.
4. Establish cozy-mystery pen name.
5. Create product folders and naming conventions.
6. Create research tracker.
7. Create QA checklists.
8. Create prompt/version-control discipline.

### Stage 1 — Puzzle pilot
Create and publish:
- A1 — Bible Word Search Large Print
- A2 — Women of the Bible — Large Print Word Search
- A3 — Psalms, Proverbs & Faith — Large Print Word Search

Evaluate after ~90 days.

### Stage 2 — Cozy pilot
Before launching B1:
- build the series bible
- define the town and recurring cast
- outline B1 and B2 fully
- architect B3 substantially
- map at least 6–8 possible books

Then publish:
- B1 — series entry
- B2 — read-through validation
- B3 — series validation

### Stage 3 — Scale or kill
Scale only where data supports it.

Puzzle expansion:
- A4 — Old Testament Stories
- A5 — New Testament Stories
- A6 — Heroes of Faith

Cozy expansion:
- B4–B6 in the same series if read-through is strong.

## Portfolio rule

Do not launch multiple unrelated pen names or random low-content products.

The company loop is:

**Research → Product hypothesis → Create → QA → Publish → Measure → Improve / Scale / Kill**

## Common rules

- Market research happens before production.
- Analyze at least 20–30 comparable titles for each new product line.
- Never copy or closely paraphrase competitors.
- Check trademarks, copyright, logos, characters, celebrity likenesses, song lyrics, sports branding, and modern Bible translations before use.
- Metadata must accurately describe the product.
- One primary audience per product.
- One clear commercial promise per product.
- Use automated QA and human QA.
- Order physical proofs for print products.
- Track units, KU pages, royalties, ads, reviews, BSR, keyword position, conversion changes, and profit per human hour.
- Disclose AI-generated text/images/translations in KDP when required by current KDP policy.

## What we are not prioritizing yet

- generic journals/planners
- generic self-help
- public-domain reprints
- children’s picture books
- hockey romance
- mafia/dark romance
- psychological thrillers
- LitRPG
- broad educational workbooks

These remain Phase-2 candidates, not current production commitments.

## Ultimate metric

**Net contribution profit ÷ human hours invested**

Revenue alone is not the objective.
