# Puzzle Book Specifications

## Product line

Christian/Bible large-print word-search books.

Initial authorization:
- A1 — Bible Word Search Large Print
- A2 — Women of the Bible — Large Print Word Search
- A3 — Psalms, Proverbs & Faith — Large Print Word Search

Scale only if the pilot works:
- A4 — Old Testament Stories
- A5 — New Testament Stories
- A6 — Heroes of Faith

## Core product promise

**Actually readable large-print faith puzzles with clean layouts, accurate solutions, and meaningful themes.**

## Target audience

Primary:
- Christian adults
- seniors
- gift buyers
- church/community buyers
- people who enjoy relaxing word-search books

Do not simultaneously design for children, advanced puzzlers, teachers, and seniors.

## Format

Initial format: **paperback**

Starting specification:
- trim: 8.5 × 11 in
- black-and-white interior
- white paper unless proof testing suggests otherwise
- target page count: 96–108
- target puzzle count: 75–85
- approximately one puzzle per page
- starting grid size: 15×15 or 16×16
- approximately 12–18 hidden words per puzzle
- word-list type size: roughly 18–22 pt
- large, high-contrast puzzle letters
- clear solution section

## Difficulty mix

Starting target:
- 70% easy to medium
- 20% medium
- 10% challenging

The experience should feel relaxing, not punishing.

## Puzzle-generation workflow

1. AI proposes puzzle topic and candidate vocabulary.
2. Validate spelling, duplicates, word length, and invalid characters.
3. Generate the puzzle algorithmically.
4. Store exact coordinates for every answer.
5. Generate the solution from stored coordinates.
6. Independently validate that every listed word exists.
7. Reject any puzzle with a missing word, duplicate answer, or broken layout.
8. Human-check readability, relevance, and factual accuracy.

Do **not** create puzzle grids with an image generator.

## Cover rules

Thumbnail recognition matters more than artistic complexity.

Hierarchy:
1. BIBLE / clear subject
2. WORD SEARCH
3. LARGE PRINT
4. benefit or puzzle count
5. visual theme

Avoid:
- tiny titles
- too many fonts
- busy collages
- competitor imitation
- misleading claims
- unlicensed artwork

## Pricing

Starting test range:
**$11.99–$12.99**

Recalculate actual royalty and printing cost before publication. Do not price by copying competitors.

## Pilot success gate

Evaluate after about 90 days.

### Green
- any one title reaches ~30+ units/month organically or with profitable ads, or
- the 3-book catalog reaches ~75+ units/month combined with improving trend

Action: create A4–A6.

### Yellow
Best title is roughly 10–29 units/month.

Action: improve cover, subtitle, price, description, keywords, preview, and ad targeting before expanding.

### Red
All three remain below roughly 10 units/month with no meaningful improvement.

Action: stop expansion and test a different puzzle micro-niche.
