# Research Findings and Decision Rationale

## Core conclusion

The best beginner strategy is a barbell:
- **fast-to-test print catalog**
- **high-lifetime-value fiction IP**

We therefore selected:
1. Christian/Bible large-print word-search books
2. Cozy mystery series

## Why large-print puzzles

The category offers:
- fast production
- strong AI/software leverage
- evergreen demand
- print-native customer experience
- low factual risk
- easy sequel/catalog structure
- evidence that relatively low-review entrants can still sell

The business advantage comes from building a reusable puzzle engine and serving one audience repeatedly.

## Why Christian/Bible as the first puzzle micro-niche

It combines:
- evergreen interest
- gifting behavior
- senior/adult audience fit
- clear thematic expansion
- lower apparent review pressure than generic word-search categories
- obvious product-series structure

The product must avoid the common failure mode of claiming “large print” while using cramped grids and tiny word lists.

## Why cozy mystery as the first fiction pilot

Cozy offers:
- long series potential
- recurring-world economics
- lower apparent review pressure than several major romance subgenres
- manageable book lengths
- Kindle Unlimited suitability
- strong fit for AI-assisted continuity and clue tracking

The repeat-reader model is the key advantage.

## Why not start with generic low-content books

Generic notebooks/planners/journals are:
- easy to produce
- easy to copy
- difficult to differentiate
- weak in repeat-purchase economics

Ease of production alone is not an advantage.

## Why not start with the largest fiction markets

Large-demand romance niches such as mafia, hockey, dark romance, and broad paranormal can be extremely competitive.

They remain future opportunities, but the first fiction pilot should maximize:
**learning + quality control + manageable competition + series architecture**

## Phase-2 candidates

After one first-line engine validates:
1. adult coloring micro-franchise
2. paranormal/shifter romance
3. targeted educational workbooks
4. horror
5. LitRPG/progression fantasy

Do not unlock a new category merely because it is interesting. It must beat current opportunities on expected lifetime profit per human hour.
