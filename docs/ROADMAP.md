# Release Roadmap and Decision Gates

## Year-1 principle

Research broadly. Execute narrowly.

Do not create all 12 books blindly.

## Phase 0 — Setup

- KDP account/tax/banking
- puzzle brand
- fiction pen name
- folder/version system
- research tracker
- QA system
- prompt library
- sales dashboard

## Phase 1 — Puzzle pilot

Create:
1. A1 Bible Word Search Large Print
2. A2 Women of the Bible
3. A3 Psalms, Proverbs & Faith

Launch as a coherent brand.

Evaluation window: approximately 90 days.

### Scale trigger
If the pilot is green, create:
4. A4 Old Testament Stories
5. A5 New Testament Stories
6. A6 Heroes of Faith

### Stop trigger
If all three show weak demand after adequate testing, do not create A4–A6. Research the next puzzle micro-niche.

## Phase 2 — Cozy pilot

Before B1 launch:
- B1 complete
- B2 preferably complete
- B3 substantially architected
- series bible complete
- 6–8 book opportunity map complete

Publish:
1. B1
2. B2 after ~3–5 weeks
3. B3 after another ~3–5 weeks

Measure read-through.

### Scale trigger
Strong read-through → produce B4–B6.

### Stop/fix trigger
Weak read-through → fix packaging/story system before producing more.

## Phase 3 — Resource allocation

After both pilots have data, choose one of four states:

1. puzzles winning / fiction winning
   - scale both, but allocate more hours to higher profit/hour

2. puzzles winning / fiction weak
   - scale puzzle catalog
   - fix or replace fiction concept

3. fiction winning / puzzles weak
   - deepen cozy series
   - test a different fast-catalog niche later

4. both weak
   - stop production
   - diagnose research, packaging, quality, and traffic assumptions before launching a new line

## Phase 4 — New experiments

Only after Phase 1/2 data:
- adult coloring
- paranormal romance
- targeted education
- horror
- LitRPG

Each new category starts with a small pilot, never a full catalog.

## Operating metrics

For every book:
- production hours
- units
- KU pages
- gross royalty
- contribution profit
- ad spend
- reviews
- rating
- read-through where applicable
- profit per production hour

## Decision rule

**Winners get sequels.**
**Promising titles get optimization.**
**Losers stop consuming capital.**
